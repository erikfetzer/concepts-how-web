SI prefixes

quecto → quetta

Any metric unit takes these prefixes. Ronna, quetta, ronto and quecto were added in November 2022, the first new prefixes since 1991, largely because global data storage was heading toward the yottabyte.

Definitions follow the SI Brochure (9th edition, 2019) of the International Bureau of Weights and Measures (BIPM). Imperial figures are rounded to about six significant digits; the inch has been exactly 2.54 cm and the pound exactly 0.45359237 kg since 1959.
